My name is Camilo Hernández and for many years I worked in a government company, but I was becoming increasingly dissatisfied with my job. The bureaucracy and lack of meaning in my work led me to question my life and purpose.

One day, while searching for answers, I stumbled upon thermodynamics and entropy. These concepts seemed fascinating to me and I realized that they could be applied to many aspects of my life, from mechanics to human behavior and society in general.

As I delved deeper into the study of thermodynamics and entropy, I discovered that these principles also applied to open and closed systems in computer science and software development. I fell in love with the idea of creating efficient and effective systems and began to devote myself to studying programming. I decided to take a new path in my career and become a full-stack developer. I started studying with dedication and discipline, applying the principles of thermodynamics and entropy to my work in programming. As I improved my skills, I felt more fulfilled and happy in my new path.

Finally, I found my purpose in life and became a passionate developer, helping others solve problems and create efficient and effective solutions. I felt useful and meaningful in my work and found the happiness I had longed for. Now, I can say that I live a very happy life, helping others with their problems thanks to my passion for the study of open and closed systems.
